Methodology

Direction basis: all traffic values shown on the map represent the total across both road directions combined. Weekday estimates keep the same two-way basis.

Colours: fixed cyclist-oriented annual-average traffic bands are identical in both countries. Optional truck adjustment keeps 5–10% as a warning only, moves roads one band worse at 10–15% trucks, two bands worse at 15–25%, and three bands worse at 25% or more.

Slovakia: official SSC CSD 2022/2023 sections, pre-aggregated for faster loading. Direction records are combined by road + counting section before publication; geometry is lightly simplified for web display. Weekday estimates use SSC weekly-variation factors; Class II/III profile can be selected above.

Hungary: Magyar Közút 2024 annual-average traffic joined to TeraLite geometry. Weekday values are comparative estimates from the section traffic-character profile. Source type, last count year and accuracy remain available in the technical footer.
