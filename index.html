Methodology

RPDI shown here is the combined traffic for both available directions of each counting section. The source directional records are grouped by road + cisloSU and their s/t/o/m values are summed. For Monday–Sunday the dashboard calculates an estimated weekday intensity by multiplying non-truck and truck RPDI separately by SSC weekly-variation factors. These weekday values are therefore modelled, not direct daily observations for every road section.

The 2022/2023 census covered all class I and II roads but only part of class III. Sections explicitly identified by the source as 2023 measurements are excluded where a year/note field permits that identification.

Weekday coefficients in this viewer are digitised approximations from the SSC 2020 weekly-variation charts presented by Dolinayová (2023); therefore use weekday values as comparative estimates rather than engineering-design inputs.
